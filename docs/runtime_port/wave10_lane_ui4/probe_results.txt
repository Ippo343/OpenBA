  The LEDGER line is the one that changed this lane's conclusions. It is
  attributed per implementation on purpose: +1 from ours and +1 from theirs,
  SYMMETRICALLY. That symmetry is what proved the two agree on refcount
  behaviour as well as on bytes, and it is what falsified this lane's first
  theory (that the entry array copied bitwise and retail left a dangling
  pointer). The disassembly of the element copier at 0x18004be97..0x18004bed0
  then confirmed the ledger: `INC dword ptr [RAX-0x4]` at 0x18004beac --
  the array element COPY-CONSTRUCTS the String and owns its reference.
