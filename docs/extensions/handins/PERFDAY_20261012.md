  cycles in every sim function on this box; the one real miss spot was the draw-item gather, now

  skinning 15% (bones of every drawn character; partly behind `render_culling`).
- **Gate hygiene**: park `WinData/*.pkm` before every gate (a mounted mod = +1 load and an 86-byte
  resave reorder); castmetric markers on every new cast; a crashed uProf translation poisons its
