## 2. What it says about the next ideas

- **The sim side at speed 50 is the snapshot walk (21%) + the walk batch (13%)**, both already prefetched and batched; the
  remaining per-candidate lambda misses (26% L1) are the candidate's component bytes themselves. No cheap idea is left there.
- **The pathfinding workers are now a real share (36% of process ops).** `FastPaths::Searcher::Run` at 36 cycles/op, 4% L1
  miss: ALU-bound open-set work. At speed 50 the workers keep up (no stall seen in the sim column), so this is throughput on
  other cores, not frame time. Only an algorithmic change to the search (a better heuristic, a cheaper open set) would move it.
- **The draw side**: the cull is the fast body everywhere; the transform lerp is closed; the sort census is profile-only.
  The next draw target would be the gather lambdas and `AddDrawItem` (the 42%-miss site of the 2026-10-12 pass is now ~2.6%).
- Housekeeping: collect at `COMPPROF=0` next time.
